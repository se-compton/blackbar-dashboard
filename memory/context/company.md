## Reporting conventions
- Quarterly deck pages: cover, results, bridge, department, market ops and fee by month, attorney production (pre-lit table plus litigation as one line), realization by location, YTD and pacing.
- Condensed P&L: one page, Q1 to Q3 and YTD with percent of revenue, every subtotal tied to the QBO export, footnotes for the litigation pool inside the fee split and the management bonus placeholder.
- Source of record is the QBO export used for the package. Prior quarters are not restated.
